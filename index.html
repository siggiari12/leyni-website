The mystique of Leyni

A quiet secret, hidden in silk.

The scarves are designed by Icelandic artist Margrét Júlíana Sigurðardóttir.

The patterns draw inspiration from Iceland’s rugged nature and the interplay of deep and vibrant colours found in the long northern summer nights, where twilight can linger for hours.

Combined with a touch of playfulness and surprise, the Leyni experience becomes a magical journey into the unknown.
