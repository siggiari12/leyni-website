The mystique of Leyni

A quiet secret, hidden in silk.

Every Leyni scarf is designed by Icelandic artist Margrét Júlíana Sigurðardóttir. The patterns draw on Iceland's rugged nature and the interplay of deep and vibrant colours found in the long northern summer nights.

Combined with a touch of playfulness and surprise, the Leyni experience becomes a magical journey into the unknown.
